After the CASPAR entry requirement is met, the one-pager says a total of at least 3 points is used in the criteria. The source lists several features.

- Current skin or scalp psoriasis counts for 2 points. A personal history or family history can count for 1 point in the situations shown in the table.
- Nail dystrophy counts for 1 point. Nail dystrophy means changes such as pitting, separation of the nail from the nail bed, or thickening under the nail.
- A negative rheumatoid factor, or RF, counts for 1 point.
- Current or previously documented dactylitis counts for 1 point. Dactylitis is swelling of an entire finger or toe.
- The source also lists new bone formation next to a joint on an X-ray as a 1-point feature.

The criteria combine these findings rather than relying on only one feature.
