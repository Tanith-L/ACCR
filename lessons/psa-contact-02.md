Two of the source materials call attention to symptoms outside the joints.

For the eyes, the clinical-manifestations poster lists anterior uveitis. Uveitis means inflammation inside the eye. The poster describes redness, pain, and sensitivity to light. The PsA one-pager also lists uveitis as a related condition and says to coordinate with ophthalmology, the medical specialty for eye care.

For the gut, the clinical-manifestations poster describes inflammation similar to inflammatory bowel disease, or IBD. It lists pain, bloating, or changes in bowel habits. The PsA one-pager also lists IBD, including Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis, as a related condition that can influence treatment choices.

These descriptions explain why the care team may ask about eye and gut symptoms as part of the full PsA picture.
