Psoriatic arthritis, or PsA, is listed in the provided RheumNotes materials as an autoimmune rheumatic disease that is strongly linked to psoriasis. Psoriasis is a condition that can cause inflamed, scaly skin.

The clinical-manifestations poster shows that PsA inflammation can affect joints, skin, nails, eyes, and the gut. Inflammation means swelling and irritation in body tissues. The poster also notes that inflammation may lead to lasting damage if it is left untreated.

PsA does not look the same in every person. The provided materials show several patterns, including joint inflammation, spine or sacroiliac-joint inflammation, tendon-attachment inflammation, whole-finger or whole-toe swelling, skin psoriasis, and nail changes. These patterns are organized more fully in later lessons.
