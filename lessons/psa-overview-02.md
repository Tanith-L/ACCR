The RheumNotes one-page PsA overview shows that inflammation can appear in many places. It highlights the spine and sacroiliac joints, shoulders, hips, elbows, wrists, hands, fingers, knees, ankles, and feet. It also shows skin or psoriasis, the eyes, and the gut.

The PsA and AS symptoms poster makes the same point visually. It marks the eyes, spine, shoulders, elbows, hips, gut, wrists, hands, fingers, skin, knees, ankles, and feet. The poster says that inflammation can strike throughout the body and can lead to long-term damage.

This broad pattern is important because PsA is not only a hand or knee problem. The source materials organize these different areas into disease domains so that the full pattern can be considered together.
