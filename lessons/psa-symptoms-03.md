A comorbidity is another health condition that is present along with PsA. The provided RheumNotes poster lists ten common comorbidities and gives percentages from the source used to make that poster.

The poster lists hypertension at 34%, metabolic syndrome at 29%, obesity at 27%, hyperlipidemia at 24%, and cardiovascular disease at 19%. It also lists type 2 diabetes at 13%, pulmonary disease at 12%, depression at 12%, thyroid disease at 11%, and gastrointestinal disease at 10%.

The poster describes hypertension as high blood pressure and hyperlipidemia as elevated cholesterol or triglycerides. Metabolic syndrome is described as central obesity together with hypertension, abnormal blood fats, and insulin resistance. These percentages belong to the provided poster and may depend on how comorbidities were defined in its source studies.
