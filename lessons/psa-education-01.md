The Four Pillars of Arthritis organizes daily-life factors into four connected areas: nourishment, movement, mastery of the mind, and sleep.

The chapter describes nourishment as the food and nutrients that support the body. Movement is presented as maintenance for strength and function. The mind is described as a command center that affects attention, stress, and how a person responds to symptoms. Sleep is presented as a major period of restoration.

The source treats these areas as parts of one system rather than isolated habits. It says the pillars reinforce one another. For example, pain or poor sleep may make movement harder, while movement, nutrition, and mental practices can influence how a person experiences daily life with arthritis.

This is a general arthritis framework from the approved materials. It is educational and does not replace PsA medical treatment.
