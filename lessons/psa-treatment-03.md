The provided PsA mind map includes minimal disease activity, or MDA, as a treatment target. MDA is a combined way of looking at several parts of disease activity rather than relying on one symptom.

The mind map notes seven areas used in the MDA framework: tender-joint count, swollen-joint count, enthesitis count, skin activity, patient pain, the patient's global rating of disease activity, and physical function. It notes meeting 5 of the 7 criteria.

The preface to Murphy's Laws of Arthritis also names MDA as a validated composite index for psoriatic arthritis. A composite index is a score or target that combines several kinds of information.

This framework helps explain why PsA follow-up can include joints, skin, tendon-attachment pain, function, and the patient's own experience at the same time.
