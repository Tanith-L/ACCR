Nourishment is the first pillar in The Four Pillars of Arthritis. The chapter discusses whole foods, protein, fats, complex carbohydrates, vitamins, minerals, and plant compounds as parts of the body's nutritional environment. It also stresses that single supplements are not disease-modifying drugs.

Sleep is the fourth pillar. The source describes sleep as a period when the body restores tissues, regulates the nervous system, and resets parts of immune and inflammatory activity. It connects disrupted sleep with worse pain, fatigue, and reduced ability to use the other pillars.

Near the end of the chapter, the four pillars are brought together: nutrition supplies materials and signals, movement supports structure and function, the mind shapes attention and coping, and sleep helps consolidate recovery.

This summary keeps the source's framework without giving a diet, supplement dose, or sleep prescription.
