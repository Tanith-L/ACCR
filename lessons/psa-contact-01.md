The provided PsA and ankylosing spondylitis symptoms poster gives a direct patient message: talk to your healthcare provider if you notice symptoms in the areas shown.

Those areas include the eyes, spine, shoulders, elbows, hips, gut, wrists, hands and fingers, skin or psoriasis, knees, ankles, and feet. The clinical-manifestations poster adds examples such as joint pain or swelling, stiffness in the spine or sacroiliac joints, a whole swollen finger or toe, tendon-attachment pain, nail changes, and inflamed skin plaques.

The purpose of this topic is to help a patient recognize which kinds of changes the provided materials connect with PsA and bring them to the care team's attention. The source materials do not provide a detailed emergency-triage list, so this lesson does not add one.
