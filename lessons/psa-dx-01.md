The provided PsA one-pager shows the CASPAR criteria, a set of classification criteria for psoriatic arthritis. CASPAR stands for Classification Criteria for Psoriatic Arthritis.

Before points are counted, the source shows an entry requirement: established inflammatory articular disease. In the one-pager, this can involve a joint, the spine, or an enthesis. An enthesis is the place where a tendon or ligament attaches to bone.

The handwritten PsA mind map repeats this structure: first establish inflammatory articular disease involving a joint, spine, or enthesis, and then consider the CASPAR point features. The point features are covered in the next lesson.
