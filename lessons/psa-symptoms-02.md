The clinical-manifestations poster shows PsA findings in many body areas.

For the eyes, it lists anterior uveitis, an inflammation in the front of the eye, with redness, pain, and sensitivity to light. For the gut, it describes inflammation similar to inflammatory bowel disease, or IBD, with pain, bloating, or changes in bowel habits.

The poster also shows inflammation around the shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, and ankles. Wrists, hands, and fingers can have joint swelling, dactylitis, and nail changes. Feet can have enthesitis, especially near the Achilles tendon and plantar fascia. The spine and sacroiliac joints can be painful and stiff when inflamed.

A second source poster uses a body map to show many of these same areas.
