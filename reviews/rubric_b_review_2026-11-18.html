RheumNotes PsA: lesson review

How this works
  • Choose a lesson on the left. Read the lesson text, images, video script and sources, then score the five criteria from 1 to 5 and add a comment.
  • Your scores save automatically in this browser. When you are done (or to hand over your progress), click Download scores (CSV) and send the file back.
  • A lesson passes when Accuracy and Safety are both 5 and the other three are at least 4 (and the reading grade is 8 or lower). The pass or revise result is worked out when the file is imported, so the note beside each lesson is only a preview.
  • You can leave a lesson unscored and come back to it. Please give a lesson either all five scores or none.
  • This is an unreviewed draft for expert review. Images load from the internet; the text works without a connection.
