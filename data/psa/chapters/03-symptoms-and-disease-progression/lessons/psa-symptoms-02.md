# Eyes, gut, and movement

AI-generated draft. Not clinically reviewed or approved.

## Page 1

PsA can affect the eyes and gut as well as the joints. Eye changes can include redness, pain, and being sensitive to light. Your gut may feel painful or bloated. You may also notice a change in your bowel habits. The shoulder joint and the tissues around it can become sore and inflamed.

## Page 2

PsA can cause pain in the hips and make it harder to move. Knees can become swollen, warm, and stiff. The ankle and the middle of the foot can become inflamed. This can cause pain and make walking hard. Elbow joints and nearby tendons can become inflamed and tender.
