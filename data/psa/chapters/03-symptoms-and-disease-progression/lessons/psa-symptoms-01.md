# Changes in skin, nails, and joints

AI-generated draft. Not clinically reviewed or approved.

Lesson ID: `psa-symptoms-01` | Topic: `symptoms` | About 1 minute(s)

## Page 1

Psoriatic arthritis, or PsA, can affect your skin, nails, and joints. Skin patches may have silvery scales. Your nails may have small pits. A whole finger or toe may swell. Your hands may have swollen joints.

## Page 2

PsA can cause back pain and stiffness. The places where tendons or ligaments attach to bone can become inflamed. In the feet, this can happen where tendons attach to bone. Ankles can become painful, and walking may be hard. Knees can feel swollen, warm, and stiff.

## Key takeaways

- A whole finger or toe may swell.
- PsA can cause back pain and stiffness.
- The places where tendons or ligaments attach to bone can become inflamed.
