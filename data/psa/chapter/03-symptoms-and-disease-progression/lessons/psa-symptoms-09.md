# Elbows and nearby tendons

AI-authored draft. Source-faithfulness and clinical review pending.

## Page 1

PsA can cause pain or tenderness in an elbow joint or nearby tendons. The elbow card shows these areas. Bending your arm or lifting an object may feel harder. Pain near an elbow and a skin patch on the elbow are different types of change.

## Page 2

Tell your team whether the problem is in the skin, inside the joint, or around it. You do not have to know the medical name. Describe where it hurts, when it started, and what you find hard to do. An exam can help the team work out which tissues are involved.

## Key takeaways

- You do not have to know the medical name.
- Pain near an elbow and a skin patch on the elbow are different types of change.
