## Page 1

The knees card describes swelling, warmth, and stiffness from inflammation. A knee may also hurt. Bending the knee, using stairs, or getting out of a chair may be harder. Knee symptoms can have more than one cause. A picture is only a guide to the area.

## Page 2

Tell your care team when the knee problem began and whether one or both knees are affected. Note any swelling and the tasks that have changed. The team may examine how the knee moves and check for other causes. Seek urgent medical help if symptoms are severe or you feel acutely unwell.
