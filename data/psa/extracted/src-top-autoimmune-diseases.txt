Top 25 Autoimmune Diseases Treated by Rheumatologists 
(ranked primarily by approximate US/global prevalence; 
incidence noted where data are available).

[page 2]
This list focuses on autoimmune rheumatic diseases (ARDs) or 
those with prominent rheumatologic manifestations that 
rheumatologists diagnose and manage (inflammatory 
arthritides, connective tissue diseases, and vasculitides). 
Ranking draws from recent US EHR studies, GBD analyses, and 
epidemiological reviews. Prevalence is the dominant metric for 
“most common” in these chronic conditions; annual incidence is 
typically much lower (e.g., 5–50 new cases per 100,000 
adults/year for many). Numbers are approximate due to 
diagnostic/coding variations and underdiagnosis.  
Top Tier (Highest Prevalence) 
1.  Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) — US ~2.5M; Global ~17–
20M+. Incidence ~20–50/100k py. Most common by far. 
2.  Psoriatic arthritis (PsA) — US ~0.5–1M+; Global ~0.135% 
prevalence (several million). Incidence ~12/100k py. 
Strongly linked to psoriasis. 
3.  Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) — US ~0.99M; 
Global hundreds of thousands to millions. Incidence ~5–
10/100k py (higher in some groups). 
4.  Axial spondyloarthritis (axSpA, incl. ankylosing 
spondylitis) — US several hundred thousand+ (broader 
estimates higher); Global variable (high in HLA-B27 
populations). Incidence varies widely. 
5.  Sjögren’s syndrome — US ~0.62M; Global low millions in 
broader estimates. Often overlaps with RA/SLE.

[page 3]
6.  Polymyalgia rheumatica (PMR) — US ~0.51M; Global 
higher in Northern European ancestry older adults. 
Frequently with GCA. 
Mid Tier 
7.  Systemic sclerosis (scleroderma) — US ~0.22M; Global 
rarer (~0.01–0.03%). 
8.  Inflammatory myopathies (dermatomyositis, 
polymyositis, etc.) — US ~0.1M combined; Global rare 
(several per 100k). 
9.  Giant cell arteritis (GCA, often with PMR) — US tens to 
low hundreds of thousands; Global similar age/ancestry 
patterns. 
10.  Mixed connective tissue disease / overlap syndromes 
— Variable; overlaps common. 
11.  ANCA-associated vasculitis (GPA, MPA, EGPA) — US/ 
Global tens of thousands each; collective higher. 
12.  Behçet’s disease — US low tens of thousands; Global 
higher in Silk Road regions. 
13.  Adult-onset Still’s disease — Rare; US/ Global 
thousands. 
14.  Relapsing polychondritis — Very rare. 
15.  Sarcoidosis (with rheumatologic features; sometimes 
managed by rheum) — US higher prevalence; Global 
variable.
