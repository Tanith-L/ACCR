RHEUMNOTES©
 Image Licensing Summary
 Anatomy Component White Paper and Anatomical Chart
Bottom line
No permission from the original sources is needed. Every image in the RheumNotes© Anatomy
Component white paper and chart was selected because its license already allows reuse, including
commercial use. Each license carries conditions, summarized below, that must be followed wherever
the images are used.
What was used
The materials contain 78 real, openly licensed or public-domain images from Wikimedia Commons
(sources include OpenStax, Gray's Anatomy, Sobotta 1909, BodyParts3D, Servier Medical Art and
Mikael Häggström), plus 21 original drawn diagrams of exam maneuvers, each clearly labeled as an
illustration. One image carrying a non-commercial license tag was deliberately excluded.

How attribution is handled
Every image caption carries a short credit, and the Image Credits appendix of the white paper lists
each file's title, author, license and source URL. These credits must stay with the images wherever
they are reused.
Guidance for the app build
 Include an Image Credits screen (or equivalent) in the RheumNotes© app that carries the attributions
for any image displayed.
 Keep CC BY-SA images unmodified, or release any edited versions under CC BY-SA.
 Where RheumNotes© wants to own an adapted image outright, replace the CC BY-SA image with a
public-domain image or original artwork.
 Before public launch, legal counsel will review the credits appendix.
