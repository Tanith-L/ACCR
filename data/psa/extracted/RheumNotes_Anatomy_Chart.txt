1. Neck (Cervical Spine)
Images (Wikimedia Commons): BodyParts3D / Anatomography (DBCLS), CC BY-SA 2.1 jp; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Atlas (C1) and axis (C2) with the dens; C3-C7 vertebral bodies; Spinous and
transverse processes; Occiput (base of skull)
■ Joints: Atlanto-occipital and atlanto-axial joints; Facet (zygapophyseal) joints; Uncovertebral
joints (C3-C7); Intervertebral disc joints
■ Cartilage: Intervertebral discs (annulus + nucleus); Hyaline cartilage of facet joints
■ Ligaments: Anterior and posterior longitudinal ligaments; Ligamentum flavum; Nuchal
ligament; Transverse ligament of the atlas
■ Tendons: Attachments of trapezius, levator scapulae and splenius
■ Bursae: No major named bursae
■ Muscles: Sternocleidomastoid; Upper trapezius; Levator scapulae; Scalenes; Splenius
capitis; Deep neck flexors (longus colli)
■ Nerves: Spinal cord; Cervical nerve roots C1-C8; Brachial plexus (C5-T1); Greater occipital
nerve
2. Spine (Thoracic, Lumbar, SI)
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Johannes Sobotta (Atlas, 1909), public domain; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Thoracic vertebrae T1-T12 and ribs; Lumbar vertebrae L1-L5; Sacrum and coccyx;
Ilium (pelvis)
■ Joints: Intervertebral disc joints; Facet joints; Costovertebral joints; Sacroiliac joints
■ Cartilage: Intervertebral discs; Cartilage lining the SI joints; Vertebral end plates
■ Ligaments: Anterior and posterior longitudinal ligaments; Ligamentum flavum; Interspinous
and supraspinous ligaments; Iliolumbar and sacroiliac ligaments
■ Tendons: Thoracolumbar fascia; erector spinae attachments
■ Bursae: No major named bursae
■ Muscles: Erector spinae; Multifidus; Quadratus lumborum; Abdominal wall (transversus,
obliques, rectus); Psoas major; Gluteal muscles
■ Nerves: Spinal cord (ends near L1-L2); Cauda equina; Lumbar and sacral nerve roots; Sciatic
nerve (L4-S3)
3. Shoulders
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Humerus (head, greater and lesser tuberosities); Scapula (glenoid, acromion,
coracoid); Clavicle
■ Joints: Glenohumeral joint; Acromioclavicular (AC) joint; Sternoclavicular joint;
Scapulothoracic gliding surface
■ Cartilage: Glenoid labrum (fibrocartilage rim); Hyaline cartilage of humeral head and
glenoid
■ Ligaments: Glenohumeral ligaments; Coracoacromial ligament; Coracoclavicular ligaments;
Joint capsule
■ Tendons: Rotator cuff: supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularis; Long head
of biceps
■ Bursae: Subacromial-subdeltoid bursa; Subscapular bursa
■ Muscles: Deltoid; Rotator cuff muscles; Trapezius; Serratus anterior; Pectoralis major;
Latissimus dorsi
■ Nerves: Axillary nerve; Suprascapular nerve; Musculocutaneous nerve; Brachial plexus
4. Elbows
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Distal humerus (epicondyles, trochlea, capitellum); Ulna (olecranon, coronoid);
Radius (radial head)
■ Joints: Humeroulnar (hinge); Humeroradial; Proximal radioulnar (pivot)
■ Cartilage: Hyaline cartilage of trochlea, capitellum and radial head
■ Ligaments: Ulnar (medial) collateral ligament; Radial (lateral) collateral ligament; Annular
ligament; Joint capsule
■ Tendons: Common extensor tendon (lateral epicondyle); Common flexor-pronator tendon
(medial); Distal biceps tendon; Triceps tendon
■ Bursae: Olecranon bursa; Bicipitoradial bursa
■ Muscles: Biceps brachii; Brachialis; Brachioradialis; Triceps; Wrist extensors (ECRB) and
flexors; Pronator teres, supinator
■ Nerves: Ulnar nerve (cubital tunnel); Median nerve; Radial nerve / posterior interosseous
5. Hands and Wrists
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Distal radius and ulna; 8 carpal bones (scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform,
trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate); 5 metacarpals; 14 phalanges
■ Joints: Radiocarpal (wrist); Distal radioulnar; Thumb base (1st CMC); MCP (knuckle) joints;
PIP and DIP finger joints
■ Cartilage: Triangular fibrocartilage complex (TFCC); Hyaline cartilage of all small joints; Volar
plates
■ Ligaments: Scapholunate ligament; Transverse carpal ligament (carpal tunnel roof);
Collateral ligaments of the fingers; Extensor retinaculum
■ Tendons: Flexor digitorum superficialis and profundus; Extensor tendons and extensor
hood; Abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis (1st compartment); Flexor tendon
pulleys
■ Bursae: Tendon sheaths (radial and ulnar bursae)
■ Muscles: Thenar muscles; Hypothenar muscles; Interossei; Lumbricals; Forearm flexors and
extensors
■ Nerves: Median nerve (carpal tunnel); Ulnar nerve (Guyon canal); Radial nerve (superficial
branch)
6. Hips
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Johannes Sobotta (Atlas, 1909), public domain; Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Femoral head and neck; Greater and lesser trochanters; Acetabulum (ilium, ischium,
pubis)
■ Joints: Hip (acetabulofemoral) joint; Pubic symphysis; Sacroiliac joints (see Spine)
■ Cartilage: Acetabular labrum; Hyaline cartilage of femoral head and acetabulum
■ Ligaments: Iliofemoral (Y) ligament; Pubofemoral and ischiofemoral ligaments; Ligament of
the head of the femur; Joint capsule
■ Tendons: Gluteus medius and minimus (greater trochanter); Iliopsoas; Hamstring origin
(ischial tuberosity); Adductor tendons
■ Bursae: Trochanteric bursa; Iliopsoas bursa; Ischial bursa
■ Muscles: Gluteus maximus, medius, minimus; Iliopsoas; Adductors; Quadriceps (rectus
femoris); Hamstrings; Deep rotators (piriformis)
■ Nerves: Sciatic nerve; Femoral nerve; Obturator nerve; Lateral femoral cutaneous nerve
7. Knees
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Jmarchn, CC0; Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0
■ Bones: Distal femur (condyles); Proximal tibia (plateau); Patella (kneecap); Fibular head
■ Joints: Tibiofemoral joint (medial and lateral compartments); Patellofemoral joint; Proximal
tibiofibular joint
■ Cartilage: Medial and lateral menisci (fibrocartilage); Hyaline cartilage of femur, tibia and
patella
■ Ligaments: Anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments (ACL, PCL); Medial and lateral
collateral ligaments (MCL, LCL); Patellar ligament
■ Tendons: Quadriceps tendon; Patellar tendon; Pes anserinus (sartorius, gracilis,
semitendinosus); Iliotibial band, hamstring tendons
■ Bursae: Prepatellar bursa; Infrapatellar bursae; Suprapatellar bursa; Pes anserine bursa;
Semimembranosus-gastrocnemius (Baker cyst site)
■ Muscles: Quadriceps; Hamstrings; Gastrocnemius; Popliteus
■ Nerves: Common peroneal (fibular) nerve; Tibial nerve; Saphenous nerve
8. Ankles
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Laboratoires Servier, CC BY-SA 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 3.0; OpenStax, CC BY 4.0
■ Bones: Distal tibia (medial malleolus); Distal fibula (lateral malleolus); Talus; Calcaneus
■ Joints: Talocrural (true ankle) joint; Subtalar joint; Distal tibiofibular syndesmosis
■ Cartilage: Hyaline cartilage of the talar dome and tibial plafond
■ Ligaments: Lateral: anterior talofibular (ATFL), calcaneofibular, posterior talofibular; Medial:
deltoid ligament; Syndesmotic (high-ankle) ligaments
■ Tendons: Achilles tendon; Tibialis posterior; Peroneus (fibularis) longus and brevis; Tibialis
anterior
■ Bursae: Retrocalcaneal bursa; Superficial (subcutaneous) calcaneal bursa
■ Muscles: Gastrocnemius and soleus; Tibialis anterior and posterior; Peroneal muscles
■ Nerves: Tibial nerve (tarsal tunnel); Deep and superficial peroneal nerves; Sural nerve
9. Feet
Images (Wikimedia Commons): Henry Vandyke Carter (Gray's Anatomy), public domain; Mikael Häggström, CC0; OpenStax, CC BY
4.0
■ Bones: Tarsals (talus, calcaneus, navicular, cuboid, 3 cuneiforms); 5 metatarsals; 14
phalanges; Sesamoids
■ Joints: Talonavicular and calcaneocuboid (midtarsal); Tarsometatarsal (Lisfranc);
Metatarsophalangeal (MTP); Interphalangeal joints
■ Cartilage: Hyaline cartilage of all joints; Plantar plates
■ Ligaments: Spring (calcaneonavicular) ligament; Long and short plantar ligaments; Lisfranc
ligament; Plantar fascia (aponeurosis)
■ Tendons: Flexor and extensor tendons of the toes; Peroneus longus; Tibialis posterior
insertion
■ Bursae: Bursa over the 1st MTP (bunion); Intermetatarsal bursae
■ Muscles: Intrinsic foot muscles (abductor hallucis, flexor digitorum brevis, interossei);
Extensor digitorum brevis
■ Nerves: Medial and lateral plantar nerves; Interdigital (common digital) nerves; Deep and
superficial peroneal nerves
